Standby Atlas

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Standby Atlas does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

This policy covers the Standby Atlas app for iPhone, made by Ryan Heskett. Standby Atlas is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with Southwest Airlines.

What you enter stays on your iPhone

The app saves the following only on your device, in a file inside the app:

  • Your ratings, notes, favorites and "been there" marks for each city
  • Flight counts you type in on the Plan tab
  • Your chosen travel date, starting airport and whether you last used the list or the map

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. Your iPhone may include it in its own iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings; Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Backups you choose to make

If you tap Save a backup, the app creates a file with your ratings, notes, favorites and counts and opens the iPhone share sheet. You decide where that file goes, for example Files, AirDrop or Messages. The app does not send it anywhere on its own. Restore from backup reads only the file you pick.

Internet connections the app makes

To show up-to-date information, the app downloads a few files. These requests carry no personal information from the app. Like any website you visit, these services can see your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as the type of device and browser engine) while sending the file:

GitHubThe daily Southwest flight-count file, from raw.githubusercontent.com. GitHub's privacy statement
cdnjs (Cloudflare)The code that draws the map. It is downloaded once, checked, and then kept in the app for offline use. Cloudflare's privacy policy
Google FontsThe typeface the app uses, from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google's privacy policy

Links you tap inside the app, such as Southwest flight searches, open in Safari. The websites you visit there have their own privacy policies.

No tracking

  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or social media tools are built into the app.
  • The app does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Children

Standby Atlas is a travel planning app for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example a feature that syncs data between phones, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
